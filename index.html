About me

I recently finished my MSc in Applied Physics at TU Eindhoven, with a graduation project at ASML and an internship at SMART Photonics before that. During my studies I found out that what drives me most is not one specific physics topic, but the process itself: modelling a complex system, checking how sensitive the outcome is to the assumptions, and turning that into a clear story.

I get the most energy from working through a problem with others. When you brainstorm together, something suddenly clicks because you look at it from a different angle. And when I code, the minutes easily turn into hours.

I like looking into things I haven't done before. That is how this website and the market briefing started: I wanted to better understand how AI agents work and how they could improve my workflow and projects.

Outside work

Competitive rowing

For seven years I rowed at competition level, with 8 to 12 training sessions a week next to a full-time study. On some days we trained twice and went to university in between, so I had to learn to plan.
